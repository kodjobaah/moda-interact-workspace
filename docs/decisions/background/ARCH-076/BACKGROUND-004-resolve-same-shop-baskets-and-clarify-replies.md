# Resolve same-shop basket replies and resume the original question after clarification

## Architecture

[ARCH-076 — WhatsApp sender pool and checkout reply routing](../../../architecture/ARCH-076-whatsapp-sender-pool-and-checkout-reply-routing.md). Coordination belongs to `moda_architect`.

## Objective

A shopper can have several active baskets at one merchant on the same WhatsApp sender. If the shopper writes “Can I get the red trainers tomorrow?” and one eligible basket contains red trainers, send that question to the ordinary conversation for that basket. If they write “Can I get it tomorrow?”, ask which basket they mean. If they choose the trainers, resume their delivery question there. A later, fresh message can refer to a different basket.

This task handles only its Background-local `kind: "shop-route"` branch. A model can interpret a customer's words after the shop has been established. It has no authority over shop ownership, sender selection, checkout lifecycle or provider dispatch.

The snapshot's `processRoutedInboundMessage` handles STOP and raw abuse admission before routing. Its normal text, voice and commerce-turn services all assume a conversation already exists. Its existing neutral routing guidance knows how to respond on a sender, but does not remember a question or its choices. Its moderation and commerce history readers use timestamps to determine which customer messages constitute the current objective. All three boundaries require explicit integration. In the snapshot, `RecoveryAgentContextService.getAgentContext` reads one recovery and verifies its conversation. The live Commerce host loads that same recovery, basket, customer objective and grant. Preserve that verified interface. Implement basket comparison before any call to this service or the commerce turn processor.
