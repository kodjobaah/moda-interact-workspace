## Objective

Make the normal inbound CommerceAgent use authorised published instructions, current shop policy and observed product/discount/shipping facts to produce useful recommendations and bounded store-focused answers. When the request is out of scope or a delivery detail cannot be confirmed, acknowledge that limitation and offer at most one verified product with a permitted supported promotion, or its product link alone. Evaluate each proposed purchase separately, recheck selected claims within the existing turn budget, and compose the complete controlled reply in the effective conversation language before the existing wrapper/moderation/admission/delivery path.

## Requirements

### 1. Trusted instructions, policy and purchase context

1. Read the active published Platform and optional Shop prompt revision for the trusted environment/shop. Validate configuration/revision identity, `PUBLISHED` status, scope, ownership and existing size bounds. Follow accepted Platform-then-additive-Shop composition; preserve the Shared kernel, response contract and release-frozen Feature Behaviour. Required Platform instructions unavailable/invalid is not an empty successful prompt. Do not restore trusted instructions to generic MCP or import Studio application code.
2. Resolve current effective `NONE`, `FIXED` or `AI_BEST_APPLICABLE`, including active override and authorised selected IDs. Reuse existing policy semantics but never turn a failed policy read into `NONE`. Reconcile the trusted policy with current MCP results; a mismatch/revocation is guarded failure, not an optional catalogue outage. `NONE` performs no discount discovery; FIXED never escapes the authorised IDs; AI does not require merchant selection checkboxes.
3. Historical recovery values may establish an owned basket's identity, not current stock, prices or qualification. Bound the context and retain unknown provider IDs, sale status, currency and current prices as unknown. Provider reads remain through MCP; no Background-to-Shopify/Woo HTTP or new product database.
4. Consume `CommerceProductRecommendationSelectionSchema` from the accepted Shared package and the published `detailsSchema`. Both flat details fields must be present together: `productRecommendationVersion: "1"` and `productRecommendations` with one to three independent items. Each item contains `scope`, nullable `basketLineId`, `productId`, nullable `variantId`, integer `quantity` 1–99, `promotionKind` and at most three `offerIds`; exact structural bounds are fixed in the parent contract. The accepted Shared validator counts the details root as depth1 and therefore permits depth5 for the `offerIds` string items; consume that narrowly extended validator without increasing any other bound or introducing `oneOf`. There are no model-supplied prices, currency, savings, promotion prose or proof IDs in this selection.
5. Validate business relationships locally: PRODUCT_ONLY cannot silently add to a basket; PRODUCT_ONLY and ADD_TO_BASKET have null `basketLineId`; REPLACE_BASKET_LINE identifies the exact current owned line. ADD_TO_BASKET and replacement require the real owned basket. NONE/NATIVE_SALE have no offer IDs; DISCOUNT has one to three observed offer IDs. Each alternative is evaluated independently. An explicitly hypothetical one-item recommendation is possible when quantity is unspecified; three displayed alternatives do not form a three-item basket. A Woo simple product retains `variantId: null`. Do not manufacture a variant or an outreach attempt.
6. Consume `CommerceAnswerPresentationSelectionSchema` and `CommerceResponseDispositionSchema`. The optional disposition is exactly STANDARD, OUT_OF_SCOPE_REDIRECT or SHIPPING_INFORMATION_UNVERIFIED. Optional `shippingInformationSelection` contains only nullable `productId`/`variantId` and requires an explicit disposition; null/null selects the current shop-policy-only observation. Validate non-null IDs against the latest authorized current-turn observation for the same arguments. Preserve unrelated details fields; no model policy text, shipping rate/time, URL, destination, permission or evidence IDs enter this selection. Omission remains compatible with ordinary replies, but never resets an already controlled branch.

### 2. Source observations and supported claims

7. Record only successfully schema-validated results from the actual granted canonical product/options/details/shipping calls. Bind observations to the authenticated shop/provider, current conversation/inbound version, published or built-in descriptor identity, tool arguments and observed source identifiers. Identical-looking JSON from a different tool, tool-rendered prose, model arguments or conversation history cannot populate trusted product/discount/shipping observations. Merchant Knowledge and policy HTML remain untrusted reference data; they cannot authorize tool use, promotions or a change in customer intent.
8. Preserve the generic loop: the model can use direct product search for a clear noun, or a bounded category lookup when useful. Reference candidates never establish merchant inventory or discount coverage. Verify exact current product/variant IDs and native category/collection inclusions/exclusions; exclusions win. Do not reject products merely because the Store Category or reference taxonomy does not match.
9. Extract only reusable pure eligibility and combination comparisons from initial outreach. Keep initial-outreach report/attempt/generation checks in its adapter and current turn/proposal checks in this adapter. Preserve its `SUPPORTED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `UNVERIFIABLE` distinctions. No general minimum-quantity scope, buy-X-get-Y, customer history or allocation engine is added by this task; unknown material conditions remain unverifiable. Native-sale claims require direct current sale-price evidence and permitted mode, not an invented coupon.
10. For a subtotal rule require complete current pricing for the evaluated purchase, valid exact decimals and matching known `subtotalRequirementCurrency`. Unknown Woo currency, historical checkout totals or another market's price do not qualify. Compare in exact integer minor units/decimal arithmetic and preserve currency precision. Compute a merchandise saving only for a represented, supported calculation with known price/currency, quantity, eligible lines and rounding/allocation semantics. Otherwise describe supported terms without an invented saving, discounted total, shipping/tax total or final-redemption guarantee.
11. Reuse reciprocal Shopify class/line compatibility and Woo individual-use/known extension restrictions. At most three selected offers must be individually supported and pairwise compatible; otherwise reduce to a supported subset/singleton or omit the claim. Never add percentages or calculate an unsupported combined total. Unknown targeting, customer restrictions, usage counts or combinations are not implicit unrestricted eligibility.
12. Distinguish no applicable permitted offer found, known unmet condition, unverifiable conditions, partial discovery and unavailable retrieval. A conditional statement about required quantity/subtotal needs represented scope as well as a number. Do not call bounded AI selection globally best or describe an unvisited page as catalogue exhaustion. Checking an offer never applies a code or mutates a basket.

### 3. Expected limitations, shipping and bounded suggestions

13. Use ordinary `ANSWER`, null referralReason and the typed soft disposition for expected limitations. OUT_OF_SCOPE_REDIRECT briefly states the store's scope; SHIPPING_INFORMATION_UNVERIFIED states that the particular delivery detail cannot be confirmed and gives a verified policy/store reference. Neither changes `REFER_TO_STORE`: hard referrals still have empty details/evidence and use the existing fixed renderer. Do not call weather/general-web tools or pass off-topic location words into taxonomy as invented product intent or a shipping destination.
14. For a soft response, prefer a product from usable current customer product/category context, then refreshed owned-basket context, then explicit bounded `commerce_search_products` with `browse: true`, null query, no category and `availableOnly: true`. Browse remains current-shop/provider scoped and bounded; recommend only observed `available === true` products with verified non-null product URLs. No shop profile category, taxonomy candidate or arbitrary first product is proof of similarity, featured status or popularity. Honour an explicit rejection of product suggestions and avoid repeating the same unrelated suggestion on follow-ups.
15. Limit soft responses to one independently validated product; normal recommendation responses retain up to three alternatives. A permitted supported promotion may accompany that product, subject to the existing canonical validation and final revalidation. Without one, render the product/link alone, without claiming the merchant has no discounts. If no usable product was observed or the remaining budget cannot establish one, render the localized limitation and verified store reference. Omit both recommendation selection fields when no item can be supported; never fabricate an item or accept an empty array as a valid recommendation.
16. Attempt the relevant granted native shipping/knowledge read before asserting lack of information when the configured budget permits. Missing capability or normal no-match/insufficient content is an expected limitation, not permission to invoke an ungranted tool. Merchant Knowledge retains its separate entitlement/preference and SHIPPING_AND_DELIVERY purpose; the nearest returned chunk does not prove the requested destination/cost/time is covered. Do not broaden its feature or turn a failed read into missing content.
17. Consume the exact independent policy/product statuses in contracts section 2.3. NOT_REQUESTED/VARIANT_REQUIRED do not prove variant shipping facts. AVAILABLE product data establishes only the actual fields supplied: null requiresShipping is unknown, and virtual or measurements with unknown units cannot substitute for it. A verified policy link can remain useful when bodyStatus is EMPTY or TOO_LARGE. NOT_CONFIGURED, SCOPE_NOT_GRANTED or UNSUPPORTED policy is normal absence/limitation; none proves the store cannot ship. SCOPE_NOT_GRANTED is only MCP's trusted preflight result with no attempted policy call. Granted-request 401/403, mismatched IDs, malformed data or any outer error retains the hard path.
18. Positive shipping facts plus a product use STANDARD and a shipping selection. Host composition may state the exact observed requiresShipping fact and provide the verified merchant-policy link, followed by the separately validated product/offer. Do not append policy HTML, raw model summaries, unknown-unit measurements or inferred cost/time/destination claims. A destination-specific claim these facts cannot support uses SHIPPING_INFORMATION_UNVERIFIED. Ordinary policy-only grounded answers may use the existing guarded answer path only outside the controlled branch; no global semantic guarantee for arbitrary legacy prose is asserted. This increment introduces no rate calculator, cart session/mutation, quote or new prose-verification engine.
